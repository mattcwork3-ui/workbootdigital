Your finished rebuild, full-screen. You can come back to this page anytime while your 15 minutes last.

Your business is strong. Your website is holding it back.

You have what most plumbing companies would love to have: 24/7 service and a 4.6-star rating across 3,506 Google reviews. But a customer landing on mrmikesplumbing.ca today runs into this:

  • No way to book online — just a form that promises someone will “get back to you ASAP.” After hours, that job goes to the next company on the list.
  • No answers on the page — no FAQ, no prices, no specials. Every question means a phone call, and plenty of people simply will not call.
  • Two different phone numbers on the same site — (587) 205-8273 up front, (403) 520-2040 in the footer. A customer in a hurry does not stop to figure out which one to call.
  • Dated, typo’d copy — COVID-era wording is still live, along with errors like “Signall Hill” and “Cliff Bungalw.” It undercuts a first-class operation.

What I built for you

  • One number everywhere: (403) 520-2040, front and centre, with a sticky emergency bar so it is always one tap away.
  • Online booking that finishes the job — a guided form plus a virtual assistant that walks the customer through it, with an SMS confirmation step.
  • Answers built in: a full FAQ, current specials, your Home Care Plan, and tabbed Plumbing / HVAC / Commercial services.
  • A modern, fast page that works properly on a phone — where most emergency searches happen — with clean, current copy throughout.
